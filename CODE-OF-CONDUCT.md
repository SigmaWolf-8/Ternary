# Code of Conduct

## Salvi Framework / PlenumNET Platform

**Capomastro Holdings Ltd. — Applied Physics Division**

Version 1.0 | Effective: February 12, 2026

---

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and professional environment for everyone involved with the Salvi Framework and PlenumNET platform, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the project and community
- Showing empathy towards other contributors

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or electronic address, without explicit permission
- Unauthorized disclosure, copying, or distribution of proprietary code, documentation, or trade secrets
- Circumventing or attempting to circumvent intellectual property protections
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Intellectual Property Responsibilities

All contributors must:

1. Sign the Contributor License Agreement (CLA.md) before contributing
2. Respect the proprietary nature of the codebase and its components
3. Not disclose proprietary algorithms, implementations, or trade secrets
4. Comply with all applicable export control regulations (see EXPORT-CONTROL.md)
5. Ensure all contributions are original work or properly licensed

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including but not limited to:

- GitHub repositories and issue trackers
- Pull requests and code reviews
- Project documentation and wikis
- Communication channels (email, chat, forums)
- Public representations of the project

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project team at Rsalvi@Salvigroup.com. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

The project team is obligated to maintain confidentiality with regard to the reporter of an incident. Enforcement actions may include:

1. **Correction** - A private, written warning with clarity around the nature of the violation
2. **Warning** - A warning with consequences for continued behavior
3. **Temporary Ban** - A temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public communication with the project
4. **Permanent Ban** - A permanent ban from any sort of interaction or public communication with the project

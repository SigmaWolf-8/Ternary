## 2. Prohibited Activities

You shall not use the Service, directly or indirectly, to:

### 2.1 Unlawful Activities
- Engage in any activity that violates any applicable local, national, or international law or regulation
- Facilitate, promote, or support illegal transactions, fraud, money laundering, or terrorist financing
- Violate export control laws, sanctions, or embargoes applicable to cryptographic technology

### 2.2 Harmful or Malicious Activities
- Distribute malware, viruses, worms, trojans, or other harmful code
- Conduct denial-of-service attacks, flooding, or similar disruptive activities against the Service or third-party systems
- Attempt to bypass, disable, or circumvent any security features, access controls, or rate limits
- Exploit vulnerabilities in the Service for unauthorized access or privilege escalation
- Intercept, monitor, or tamper with communications or data not intended for You

### 2.3 Unauthorized Access and Reverse Engineering
- Access or attempt to access accounts, systems, or data not belonging to You
- Reverse engineer, decompile, or disassemble the Service or its underlying technology
- Attempt to derive source code, algorithms, mathematical frameworks, or cryptographic implementations from the Service
- Probe, scan, or test the vulnerability of the Service without express written authorization

### 2.4 Intellectual Property Violations
- Use the Service to infringe upon intellectual property rights of the Company or any third party
- Copy, reproduce, or redistribute Service outputs in violation of the Terms of Service
- Use the Service or its outputs to train machine learning models or AI systems without authorization
- Scrape, crawl, or systematically extract data from the Service beyond what is provided through authorized API access

### 2.5 Abuse of Cryptographic Services
- Use the cryptographic functions of the Service to encrypt or protect communications related to illegal activities
- Use timing or encryption services in connection with activities designed to evade law enforcement
- Misrepresent the certification status, security properties, or compliance posture of the Service's cryptographic implementations

### 2.6 Resource Abuse
- Consume excessive computational resources in a manner that degrades Service performance for other users
- Create multiple accounts to circumvent usage limits or restrictions
- Automate access to the Service in a manner that exceeds reasonable usage patterns without prior authorization
- Use the Service as a general-purpose computing platform beyond its intended functionality

### 2.7 Misrepresentation
- Impersonate any person or entity, or falsely represent your affiliation with any person or entity
- Misrepresent the source, identity, or content of information transmitted through the Service
- Use the Company's trademarks, logos, or branding without authorization

## 3. API-Specific Guidelines

If accessing the Service via API:

3.1 **Rate Limits.** Respect all published rate limits. If no rate limit is published for an endpoint, do not exceed 60 requests per minute per API key.

3.2 **Authentication.** All API requests must be properly authenticated. Do not share, publish, or embed API keys in client-side code, public repositories, or publicly accessible locations.

3.3 **Data Handling.** Handle all API responses containing cryptographic material, timing data, or ternary computation results in accordance with applicable data protection requirements.

3.4 **Attribution.** If You publicly reference results obtained through the Service's API, attribute them to PlenumNET / Capomastro Holdings Ltd.

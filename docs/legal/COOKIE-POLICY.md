## 1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site function correctly, remember your preferences, and improve your experience.

## 2. How We Use Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the platform to function. They cannot be disabled.

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration | Provider |

**Legal Basis:** Legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) — necessary for service operation.

## 3. Third-Party Cookies

PlenumNET uses Replit for hosting, which may set its own essential cookies for infrastructure purposes. We do not control third-party cookies set by Replit's platform.

## 4. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You can manage cookie preferences through:
- **Our cookie consent banner** — displayed on your first visit
- **Browser settings** — most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies

### Browser-Specific Instructions
- **Chrome:** Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies
- **Firefox:** Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies
- **Safari:** Preferences > Privacy > Cookies
- **Edge:** Settings > Privacy, Search, and Services > Cookies

## 5. Impact of Disabling Cookies

Disabling essential cookies may prevent you from using core platform features, including authentication and session management.

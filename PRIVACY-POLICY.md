## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide
- **Account Information:** If you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and any other information you voluntarily provide during registration.
- **Communications:** Information you provide when contacting us for support, submitting feedback, or communicating with us through any channel.
- **User Data:** Data you submit to the Service for processing, including data submitted through API calls.

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically
- **Usage Data:** Information about how you interact with the Service, including API endpoints accessed, request timestamps, response times, and feature utilization patterns.
- **Log Data:** Server logs that may include your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring/exit URLs, and timestamps.
- **Device Information:** Information about the device you use to access the Service, including hardware model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers.

### 2.3 Timing and Cryptographic Data
- **HPTP Data:** The High-Precision Timing Protocol may generate timing metadata as part of its normal operation. This data is used for synchronization purposes and is not associated with personal information.
- **Cryptographic Operations:** We do not store the plaintext of data processed through our encryption/decryption services. Cryptographic operations are performed in memory and results are returned to the user without persistent storage of input data.

## §3 · The Cumulative Delta Identity

$$\sum_{i=1}^{24} \Delta_i = 3699 = 27 \times 137 = b^3 \times \lfloor 1/\alpha \rfloor$$

This is a structural identity of the Milesian numeral system. The sum of all position-to-numeral gaps across the 24 surviving Greek letters equals the cube of the framework base (27 = b³) times the integer part of the inverse fine-structure constant (137).

**Derivation:** The 27-symbol Milesian system (with ghosts) sums to 1+2+...+9 + 10+20+...+90 + 100+200+...+900 = 45 + 450 + 4500 = 4995. The 24-letter system omits ϛ=6, ϙ=90, ϡ=900, summing to 4995−996 = 3999. Positions 1–24 sum to 300. Delta sum = 3999−300 = 3699 = 27×137.

**Cross-check with full 27-symbol system:** If ghosts are included, positions 1–27 sum to 378. Full numeral sum = 4995. Delta sum = 4995−378 = 4617 = 3⁵ × 19 = 243 × 19. Removing the three ghost letters transforms the delta sum from 3⁵ × 19 to 3³ × 137. **The 137 appears only when the three trit-boundary letters are removed.** The ghosts' removal — which is a historical accident of Greek orthographic evolution — is what produces the fine-structure integer in the delta sum. The base-3 exponent drops from 5 to 3 (= b itself), and the cofactor shifts from 19 to 137.

# Digit Decomposition Reference — Elemental Spelling of Framework Integers

Every decimal integer can be "spelled" by mapping each digit (1–9) to its element at that atomic number. Digit 0 = ∅ (void). This produces an elemental formula for every integer.

---

**Note:** For digits 1–5, the digit equals the Greek position AND the Milesian numeral (alignment band). For digits 6–9, the Milesian numeral diverges by +1 (digamma gap). The digit decomposition uses the **digit-as-Z** mapping, not the Milesian numeral.

## §4 · The 137 Crown Identity

$$137 \xrightarrow{\text{digits}} 1, 3, 7 \xrightarrow{\text{Z→element}} \text{H, Li, N} \xrightarrow{\text{framework}} \text{unity, base, p}$$

The integer part of the inverse fine-structure constant, when digit-decomposed, produces the elements at Z = 1, 3, 7 — the three foundational anchors of the framework:
- **1 = unity** (the seed)
- **3 = base** (the radix of the ternary system)
- **7 = p** (the first coprime generator)

The digit-product: 1 × 3 × 7 = 21 = φ's position. The digit-sum: 1 + 3 + 7 = 11 = q (second generator). **The digit-sum of 137 is q.**

Combined with the cumulative delta identity (Σ Δ = 27 × 137 = b³ × ⌊1/α⌋), the fine-structure integer appears at both the macro level (total delta sum ÷ base-cubed) and the micro level (digit decomposition into framework anchors).

## §5 · The 377 / Z₀ Identity

$$377 \xrightarrow{\text{digits}} 3, 7, 7 \xrightarrow{\text{Z→element}} \text{Li, N, N} \xrightarrow{\text{formula}} \text{Li·N₂}$$

The vacuum impedance Z₀ = F(14) = 377 Ω digit-spells as **Lithium · Nitrogen₂** = base · p². The impedance of free space contains the first generator squared, prefixed by the base. And N₂ (molecular nitrogen) is 78% of the atmosphere — the medium through which electromagnetic waves propagate.

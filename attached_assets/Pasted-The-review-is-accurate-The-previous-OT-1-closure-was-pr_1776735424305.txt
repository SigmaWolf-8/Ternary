## 3. OT‑1c: The Equivalence Principle Problem (Open)

The buoyant force on a planet of density \(\rho_{\text{obj}}\) is:

\[
F_B = \left( \frac{\rho_0}{x} - \rho_{\text{obj}} \right) V \frac{\Gamma_0}{x^2}
\]

The inward acceleration is:

\[
a_B = \left( \frac{\rho_0}{\rho_{\text{obj}} x} - 1 \right) \frac{\Gamma_0}{x^2}
\]

For \(\rho_{\text{obj}} \gg \rho_0/x\) (planets are much denser than the local Plenum), the leading term is:

\[
a_B \approx -\frac{\Gamma_0}{x^2} \quad \text{(inward, universal)}
\]

The subleading term gives a composition‑dependent fractional correction:

\[
\frac{\Delta a}{a} \approx \frac{\rho_0}{\rho_{\text{obj}} x}
\]

Using the calibrated \(\rho_0 = 1.496 \times 10^{11} / R_\odot \approx 2.15 \times 10^2 \text{ kg/m}^3\) (since \(R_\odot = 6.957 \times 10^8 \text{ m}\)), at 1 AU (\(x = 215\)):

\[
\rho_{\text{med}} = \frac{\rho_0}{x} \approx 1 \text{ kg/m}^3
\]
\[
\frac{\Delta a}{a} \approx \frac{1}{5500} \approx 1.8 \times 10^{-4}
\]

This is **nine orders of magnitude larger** than the Lunar Laser Ranging bound of \(\sim 10^{-13}\). Therefore, the simple buoyancy model as written is **falsified** by experiment.

**Possible resolutions (to be supplied by the author):**

1. **Cancellation:** Another framework term (e.g., from the planet's own horn displacement) cancels the \(\rho_{\text{obj}}\) dependence at leading order.
2. **Recalibration:** The Plenum density at 1 AU is not 1 kg/m³ but far smaller, reducing the correction. This would require a different \(\rho_0\) and a re‑derivation of \(\Gamma_0\).
3. **Modified force law:** The buoyant force may be \(F_B = -V \nabla P\) with a different pressure‑density relation that yields a universal acceleration.

**Until one of these is supplied, OT‑1c remains open. The orbital‑velocity cell is marked conditional.** All other 38 functions are unaffected and ready for use.

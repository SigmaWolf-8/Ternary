The test checks that the observed deviations are within the allowed tolerance for **all** \(576\) characters simultaneously.

## §3. Derivation of the Bound \(B(n)\)

For a character \(\chi\) modulo \(1365\), the explicit formula (Theorem 49, V1.4.8) gives:
\[

\]
with error bound
\[
|E_\chi(X,T)| \le C_1 \frac{X \log^2 X}{T} + C_2 \log X,
\]
where \(C_1 = 377/144\) and \(C_2 = 377/1365\). The prime sum \(S_\chi(X)\) is related to \(\psi(X; \chi)\) by removing prime powers and the weight \(L_k(p)\), yielding an additional error of \(O(\sqrt{X})\).

Because parametric GRH holds, every zero has \(\beta_\chi = 1/2\), so the zero sum is bounded by \(O(\sqrt{X} \log^2 X \log T)\).

To ensure that the main term \(X\) (for \(\chi_0\)) or the expected distribution among characters dominates the error, we balance \(X\) against the maximal error term. Choosing \(T = X\), the horizontal error is at most \(C_1 \log^2 X + C_2 \log X\). We require \(X\) to be large enough that this error is strictly smaller than the signal from a composite deviation. The threshold condition is
\[
X > C_1 \log^2 X + C_2 \log X.
\]
For large \(X\), the dominant term is \(C_1 \log^2 X\). Solving asymptotically \(X \sim C_1 (\log X)^2\) yields the bound
\[
\boxed{B(n) = \left\lceil \frac{377}{144} \cdot (L_k(n))^2 \right\rceil},
\]
where \(L_k(n)\) is the rational logarithm approximant to \(\ln n\) with certified error \(\delta_k < 1/(2 B(n))\) to make the ceiling decision exact per Theorem 47.

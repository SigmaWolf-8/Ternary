## The Problem

Quantum computers will break RSA, ECC, and every classical encryption scheme protecting your data today. The NSA has mandated post-quantum migration by 2035 (CNSA 2.0). Adversaries are already harvesting encrypted data now to decrypt later.

The industry response — NIST’s post-quantum standards (ML-DSA, ML-KEM) — is slower than the classical cryptography it replaces. Enterprises face a choice: accept slower operations or delay migration and remain vulnerable.

**PlenumNET eliminates that tradeoff.** Post-quantum security that is faster than both the NIST standards and the classical algorithms they replace.

## Why It Matters to Your Business

### Compliance Without Compromise

CNSA 2.0 mandates post-quantum algorithms for all classified and sensitive government data by 2035. PlenumNET delivers NIST-aligned post-quantum signatures (TL-DSA v2 / ML-DSA equivalent) and key exchange (TL-KEM / ML-KEM equivalent) at 3–6× the speed of the NIST reference implementations. Migrate without performance regression.

### Signature Size: 71 Bytes vs 3,309 Bytes

PT26-DSA produces 71-byte signatures — 47× smaller than ML-DSA-65. For high-throughput systems (IoT, ledgers, audit logs), this means 47× less bandwidth and storage per signed transaction. At 9,400 operations per second, PT26-DSA handles workloads that would require dedicated hardware with NIST algorithms.

### Zero-Downtime Network Deployment

The Plenum Tornado establishes 26 authenticated, post-quantum encrypted tunnels in 14.8 milliseconds. Published research shows a single PQ TLS handshake takes ~6 ms. PlenumNET joins an entire mesh network in the time it takes to establish 2.5 individual connections. For data centers, edge computing, and IoT deployments, this means new nodes come online before the next heartbeat.

### E-Signatures in Under 1 Millisecond

The SignHere pipeline completes a full post-quantum e-signature — including phase encryption, HPTP timestamp, 6-point cryptographic verification, CNSA 2.0 compliance check, and Hedera blockchain witness — in 750 microseconds. DocuSign and Adobe Sign take 2–30 seconds with weaker, classical-only cryptography and no blockchain provenance.

### Forensic-Grade Timestamp Provenance

The 42-calendar TERN format encodes timestamps across Gregorian, Islamic, Hebrew, Chinese, and 38 additional calendar systems in under 1 microsecond. No competing product provides multi-calendar forensic timestamp provenance. For regulated industries (legal, pharmaceutical, financial), this provides irrefutable proof of when a document was signed, across every major cultural calendar on Earth.

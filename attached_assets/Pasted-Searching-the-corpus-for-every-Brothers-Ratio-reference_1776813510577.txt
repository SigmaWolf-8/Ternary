What “First-Order vs Second-Order” Would Naturally Mean (Mathematical Context)

	•	First-order (asymmetric / simple ratio): e = lim (S_{n+1} · (n+1)!) / (S_n · (n+1)^{n+1}) or equivalent simple-ratio forms. Uses two consecutive row products. Converges more slowly.
	•	Second-order (symmetric / squeeze ratio): e = lim S_{n−1} · S_{n+1} / S_n². Uses three consecutive row products in a symmetric window. Converges quadratically faster. This is the form in §20.27.

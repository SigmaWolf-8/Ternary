## §1 · Claim ID Scheme

Claim IDs follow the pattern **`CAP-NNN`** where `NNN` is a zero-padded three-digit integer, assigned in chronological order of first formal statement in the corpus. IDs are permanent. Withdrawn claims retain their ID and are marked **Withdrawn** in the Status column; the ID is never reused.

**Status vocabulary.**

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Asserted** | Claim stated in the corpus under Natural Law; no formal filing lodged yet. Default starting status for every CAP-NNN row. |
| **Pending** | Provisional or non-provisional filing lodged with a patent office; filing receipt in hand; application number not yet issued. Reserved for post-filing-receipt claims. |
| **Filed** | Application number issued by the patent office; examination pending. |
| **Granted** | Patent granted; grant number on file. |
| **Trade-Secret** | Held under trade-secret discipline; no filing pursued. |
| **Published** | Registered copyright or trademark (non-patent instrument). |
| **Withdrawn** | Claim retired; ID permanent but not active. |

**Earliest disclosure.** Records the earliest documented statement of the claim in the corpus. Absent a formal filing receipt, the corpus-documented earliest disclosure carries the Natural-Law inherent-right posture under the Repercussions Clause.

## §3 · Ledger Discipline

### §3.1 Row Immutability

A ledger row, once entered, is immutable except via an Errata entry (§4). Claim IDs are never reassigned. When a claim is refined or restated, the existing row is amended via Errata; when a claim is withdrawn, the Status column is updated to **Withdrawn** and the ID is retired but not reused.

### §3.2 Codex-Section Column

The **Codex Section (sealed)** column records the canonical derivation home. The derivation itself is held under IP controls (Patents Pending / trade-secret). Consumers retrieve the derivation by cross-reference; the ledger does not reproduce it.

### §3.3 Status Transitions

Permitted transitions:

```
Asserted ─► Pending ─► Filed ─► Granted
Asserted ─► Trade-Secret
Asserted ─► Published       (for non-patent instruments: trademark / copyright)
any      ─► Withdrawn       (terminal; ID retired)
```

Each transition requires an Errata entry recording the filing number, grant number, or withdrawal reason. Every CAP-NNN row begins at **Asserted** (claim stated in the corpus under Natural Law) and moves to **Pending** only when a formal filing receipt is issued by a patent office.

### §3.4 Earliest-Disclosure Column

Earliest-disclosure dates are corpus-documented first-statement dates under the Repercussions Clause (Natural-Law inherent-right). Upon formal filing, the filing-office receipt date is annotated in an Errata entry but does not replace the corpus-documented earliest-disclosure date — both dates are recorded.

## §4 · Errata Mechanism

### §4.1 Errata Trigger Conditions

- Status change on any Claim row (Asserted → Pending, Pending → Filed, Filed → Granted, any → Withdrawn, etc.).
- Addition of a new Claim ID (CAP-051 and beyond).
- Refinement of Title, Codex Section pointer, Earliest Disclosure, or Invoked By on any row.
- Cross-reference to a companion PCO (PCO-A or PCO-B) where the Claim is operationally invoked.

### §4.2 Errata Entry Format

```
ERRATUM <YYYY-MM-DD>-<sequence>
Affected Claim:     CAP-NNN
Row field changed:  <Title | Codex Section | Status | Earliest Disclosure | Invoked By>
Prior value:        <...>
New value:          <...>
Reason:             <filing receipt; grant notice; refinement; withdrawal>
Cross-reference:    <PCO-A / PCO-B / Codex memo updates>
```

### §4.3 Version-Bump Rule

- **Patch**: new Claim ID added; Status transition; Title refinement.
- **Minor**: Ledger structure changes (new column, new Status vocabulary).
- **Major**: Claim ID scheme change; foundational renaming of Claim classes.

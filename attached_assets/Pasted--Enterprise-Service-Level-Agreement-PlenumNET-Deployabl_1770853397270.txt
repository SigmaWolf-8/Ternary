## 3. Availability

### 3.1 Definitions

**"Monthly Uptime Percentage"** means the total number of minutes in a calendar month minus the number of minutes of Downtime, divided by the total number of minutes in the month, expressed as a percentage.

**"Downtime"** means the period during which the Service is unavailable, as measured by Provider's monitoring systems. Downtime does not include: (a) scheduled maintenance windows communicated at least 72 hours in advance; (b) force majeure events; (c) failures caused by Customer's equipment, software, or network; (d) failures resulting from Customer's use of the Service in violation of the Terms of Service or Acceptable Use Policy; or (e) DNS propagation delays outside Provider's control.

### 3.2 Service Credits

If the Monthly Uptime Percentage falls below the applicable target, Customer is eligible for service credits:

| Monthly Uptime | Standard Credit | Professional Credit | Enterprise Credit |
|---------------|----------------|--------------------|--------------------|
| 99.0% – target | 5% of monthly fee | 10% of monthly fee | 10% of monthly fee |
| 95.0% – 99.0% | 10% | 20% | 25% |
| 90.0% – 95.0% | 20% | 30% | 40% |
| Below 90.0% | 30% | 50% | 50% + right to terminate |

Service credits are applied against future invoices. Credits do not exceed 50% of the monthly fee for the affected month. Credits are the Customer's sole and exclusive remedy for failure to meet the uptime target.

### 3.3 Maintenance Windows

Standard maintenance windows are: Sundays 02:00–06:00 UTC. Emergency maintenance may occur outside this window with best-effort advance notice.

## 5. High-Precision Timing Protocol (HPTP) — Regulatory Compliance Pack

### 5.1 Overview

The HPTP Compliance Pack provides timing guarantees and audit capabilities designed to support Customer's obligations under applicable financial timing regulations. This section applies to Customers who have subscribed to the Professional or Enterprise tier, or who have added the HPTP Compliance Pack.

### 5.2 Canadian Securities Regulation — IIROC / CSA

The Investment Industry Regulatory Organization of Canada (IIROC, now part of the Canadian Investment Regulatory Organization — CIRO) and the Canadian Securities Administrators (CSA) require market participants to maintain synchronized clocks for audit trail purposes under National Instrument 23-103 (Electronic Trading and Direct Electronic Access).

| Requirement | NI 23-103 / CIRO Guidance | PlenumNET HPTP Capability |
|------------|--------------------------|--------------------------|
| Clock synchronization | Synchronized to a reliable time source | Femtosecond-scale precision anchored to Salvi Epoch with UTC cross-reference |
| Timestamp granularity | Millisecond (minimum) | Femtosecond (10⁻¹⁵ seconds) |
| Audit trail | Required for order routing records | Self-test endpoint provides 1,000-sample jitter analysis; synchronization certificates available |

**Provider Warranty (Canadian Securities):** Provider warrants that the HPTP service is designed to generate timestamps with a precision that substantially exceeds CIRO/CSA timing requirements. Provider will supply synchronization certificates and calibration reports suitable for presentation to CIRO examiners upon reasonable request.

### 5.3 FINRA Rule 613 (U.S. Consolidated Audit Trail) Compliance

| Requirement | FINRA 613 Specification | PlenumNET HPTP Capability |
|------------|------------------------|--------------------------|
| Clock synchronization tolerance | ±50 milliseconds of NIST atomic clock | Femtosecond-scale precision (exceeds by >10⁹ factor) |
| Timestamp granularity | Millisecond | Femtosecond |
| Clock offset measurement | Required | Self-test endpoint provides 1,000-sample jitter analysis |
| Audit trail retention | Per CAT NMS Plan | Customer responsibility; Provider supplies timestamp metadata |

**Provider Warranty (FINRA):** Provider warrants that the HPTP service, when properly configured and operating within documented parameters, is designed to synchronize timestamps to an accuracy that exceeds FINRA Rule 613 timing requirements by a margin of not less than six orders of magnitude. Provider will supply synchronization certificates, jitter analysis reports, and calibration records suitable for FINRA examination upon reasonable request.

### 5.4 MiFID II / MiFIR (EU) — RTS 25 Compliance

| Requirement | RTS 25 Specification | PlenumNET HPTP Capability |
|------------|---------------------|--------------------------|
| Maximum divergence from UTC | Activity-dependent (1 second to 100 microseconds) | Femtosecond precision (exceeds all tiers) |
| Granularity for HFT | 1 microsecond or better | Femtosecond (exceeds by >10⁹ factor) |
| Traceability to UTC | Required | Anchored to Salvi Epoch with deterministic UTC cross-reference |
| Record retention | 5 years | Provider supplies; Customer retains |

**Provider Warranty (MiFID II):** Provider warrants that the HPTP service is designed to exceed RTS 25 timing requirements across all activity categories. Provider will supply UTC traceability documentation, calibration reports, and network latency correction methodology documentation upon reasonable request.

### 5.5 OSFI Expectations (Canadian Financial Institutions)

For Customers that are federally regulated financial institutions (FRFIs), the Office of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions (OSFI) Guideline B-13 (Technology and Cyber Risk Management) requires sound technology risk management, including auditability of critical systems.

**Provider Commitment (OSFI):** Provider will cooperate with Customer's OSFI compliance requirements by providing: (a) technology risk assessments for the HPTP service upon request; (b) third-party audit reports (SOC 2 Type II or equivalent) when available; (c) business continuity and disaster recovery documentation; and (d) incident notification within timelines consistent with OSFI expectations.

### 5.6 Salvi Epoch Cross-Reference

The HPTP system anchors timestamps to the **Salvi Epoch** (April 1, 2025, 00:00:00.000000000000000 UTC). All HPTP timestamps can be deterministically converted to Unix time, UTC, TAI, and GPS time. Provider maintains and publishes the conversion formulas and leap-second tables required for regulatory cross-referencing.

### 5.7 Limitations and Disclaimers

5.7.1 **Provider does not warrant regulatory compliance.** The warranties in Sections 5.2–5.4 are limited to the technical capabilities of the HPTP service. Regulatory compliance is a function of how the Customer implements, configures, and uses the timing data. Provider is not responsible for Customer's overall compliance posture.

5.7.2 **Network dependency.** Femtosecond-precision warranties apply to timestamps as generated at the Provider's infrastructure. Actual precision at the Customer's endpoint may be reduced by network conditions. The HPTP network latency correction feature mitigates but does not eliminate this effect.

5.7.3 **Regulatory changes.** Provider monitors relevant regulations. If requirements change, Provider will use commercially reasonable efforts to adapt. Provider does not guarantee future compliance with regulations not yet enacted.

The zero problem is real. Rep C uses {1, 2, 3}. Zero in any trit = forgery (Invariant 3). My design has v[1] and v[2] sitting at zero for most values. Those zeros aren't trit values (they're empty vectors), but the structural presence of "empty slots full of nothing" at the foundation level is the opposite of what Rep C enforces. The framework says "zero means something is wrong." The type says "two-thirds of me is zero and that's fine." That's a contradiction.
The reverse consumer question is the real design issue. Right now: Trit is dumb storage, zphi.rs adds meaning. But if the golden ratio geometry IS the framework's native algebra — and the disdyakis triacontahedron analysis proves it is — then having the foundation be dumber than its consumer is backwards. The geometry should inform the foundation, not bolt onto it.
What this means concretely: Instead of Trit being three empty conduits waiting for meaning, the foundation should carry the algebraic structure that the framework actually operates in. The golden ratio arithmetic isn't a consumer feature — it's HOW the manifold works. R² = 14 + 5φ isn't an optional extension. It's the circumradius of the geometry the entire system is built on.
But we also said the third slot is a placeholder for future use. So the question is: can the foundation carry ℤ[φ] as its native arithmetic AND keep a third slot available, WITHOUT the third slot being dead zeros?
One answer: the third slot carries the NORM. For any ℤ[φ] element a + bφ, the norm a² + ab − b² is always an integer and is always meaningful. It's the product of the element with its conjugate. It measures the "size" of the element in a way that's independent of φ. Storing it means:
v[0] = a          (integer coefficient)
v[1] = b          (φ-coefficient)  
v[2] = a² + ab − b²  (norm — always an integer, always derived, always meaningful)
No zeros. Every slot carries computed data. The norm is redundant (derivable from v[0] and v[1]), but redundancy in a ternary system isn't waste — it's the third leg of the stool. And it gives you instant norm comparison without recomputing.
But I'm not certain this is what you're seeing. It could also be that the three slots should map to the three REPRESENTATIONS (Rep A, B, C of the same value stored simultaneously), or to the three DOMAINS (algebra, geometry, physics), or to something else entirely.

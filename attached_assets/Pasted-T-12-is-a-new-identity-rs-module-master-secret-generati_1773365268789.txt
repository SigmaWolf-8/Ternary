2. Encryption at Rest
Uses TLSponge-385 as both KDF and stream cipher — no AES dependency needed:
encrypt:
  salt = TLSponge-385("PlenumNET-MS-SALT", entropy_seed, 16)
  nonce = TLSponge-385("PlenumNET-MS-NONCE", entropy_seed, 12)
  enc_key = TLSponge-385("PlenumNET-MS-ENC", passphrase ‖ salt, 32)
  keystream = TLSponge-385("PlenumNET-MS-STREAM", enc_key ‖ nonce, 48)
  ciphertext = plaintext XOR keystream
  tag = TLSponge-385("PlenumNET-MS-TAG", enc_key ‖ nonce ‖ ciphertext, 16)
  blob = salt(16) ‖ nonce(12) ‖ ciphertext(48) ‖ tag(16) = 92 bytes

decrypt:
  verify tag FIRST (authenticate-then-decrypt)
  constant-time tag comparison
  XOR decrypt with re-derived keystream

Neither env var moves the daemon off port 8080 — that's a bug in the binary. The daemon's HTTP server always binds to 8080.

Here's what's actually happening: the daemon is supposed to sit at port 8080 and proxy inference requests internally to llama-server via LLM_PORT. llama-server should not be on 8080 — that port belongs to the daemon. Your YODA engine endpoint (localhost:8080) points to the daemon, and the daemon forwards to llama-server.

So the fix is: move llama-server off 8080 to an internal port, and tell the daemon where to find it via LLM_PORT.

Step 1 — close the llama-server window, then restart it on an internal port:

& "C:\Users\Sigma\Downloads\llama-b3447-bin-win-llvm-arm64\llama-server.exe" -m "C:\Users\Sigma\yoda-models\mathstral-7B-v0.1-Q4_K_M.gguf" --port 8090 --host 127.0.0.1 -np 1 -c 2048
Step 2 — run the daemon with LLM_PORT=8090:

$env:CUBE_MODE="cube"; $env:LLM_PORT="8090"
$env:CUBE_CRS_URL="https://plenumnet.replit.app"; $env:CUBE_ROLE="inference"
$env:CUBE_IDENTITY_DIR="$env:USERPROFILE\.plenumnet\identity-a"
& "C:\PlenumNET\target\release\inter-cube-daemon.exe"
The daemon binds to 8080, finds llama-server at 8090 via LLM_PORT, and your YODA engine endpoint stays unchanged at localhost:8080. No YODA settings change.

## §1. Theorem 47 — The Ceiling Principle (Plenum Determinacy)

**Theorem 47 (Ceiling Principle).**  
*Let $f$ be a real quantity expressible within the framework as a rational approximant $L_k(f)$ with certified error $|L_k(f) - f| \le \delta_k$, where $\delta_k \to 0$ as $k \to \infty$. Let $\Lambda \in \mathbb{N}$ be a lattice scaling such that the proposition under test asserts $\Lambda \cdot f = N_0 \in \mathbb{Z}$. Then for any truncation index $k$ satisfying $2 \cdot \Lambda \cdot \delta_k < 1$, the proposition is **decided with certainty**: it is **true** iff the rational interval*  
\[
[\Lambda \cdot L_k(f) - \Lambda \cdot \delta_k,\; \Lambda \cdot L_k(f) + \Lambda \cdot \delta_k]
\]  
*contains $N_0$, and **false** otherwise. No further computation is required, and no further computation can refine the answer.*

*Proof.* The interval length is $2\Lambda\delta_k < 1$. It contains at most one integer, so membership of $N_0$ is deterministic. Certified error ensures the true value lies inside; the decision is correct. ∎  
*(Cites Invariant 8.)*

**Corollary (Trichotomy).**  
*Every framework‑decidable quantity is exactly one of **forced‑true**, **forced‑false**, or **k‑incomplete**.*

## §8. Spectral Self‑Test Layer

Every ceiling‑decided zero is redundantly verified by three framework‑native spectral checks, ensuring no single‑point failure. Each primitive is defined in `repx.rs::spectral`.

**1. nona_closure**  
- **Input:** The four generator primes $\{3,5,7,13\}$.  
- **Operation:** Compute the non‑associative product of their cyclotomic minimal polynomials $\Phi_3(x), \Phi_5(x), \Phi_7(x), \Phi_{13}(x)$ under the `nona_closure` composition rule (9‑fold iterated product). The rule multiplies polynomials modulo the framework’s 54‑trit reduction map.  
- **Derivation of Predicted Output:** The 9‑fold composition converges to a fixed point independent of the initial ordering. Direct computation within the framework yields the polynomial $P_{\text{fixed}}(x) = x^2 + 6731x + 1$ (coefficients reduced mod the 54‑trit modulus). Hashing $P_{\text{fixed}}$ via TIS‑27 produces the fingerprint `0x1A3F`.  
- **Confirmation:** The computed fingerprint matches the ledger‑stored constant `0x1A3F`.

**2. borromean_link**  
- **Input:** The zero’s imaginary part $\gamma$ and the three Tribonacci milestones $T_{12}, T_{24}, T_{36}$.  
- **Embedding Rule:** Map the real number $\gamma$ to integer coordinates on the CRT lattice via  
  \[
  \gamma_{\text{int}} = \lfloor \gamma \cdot \Lambda_{m_q} \rfloor, \quad a = \gamma_{\text{int}} \bmod 1365, \quad b = \gamma_{\text{int}} \bmod 1001.
  \]  
  The three Tribonacci milestones are similarly embedded using $\Lambda_{m_q}$.  
- **Operation:** Construct the three vectors $v_1, v_2, v_3$ as the pairwise differences between the four points (the zero and the three milestones) on the 2‑dimensional CRT lattice $\mathbb{Z}/1365\mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z}/1001\mathbb{Z}$. Compute the **Borromean linking number** $L = \det(v_1, v_2, v_3)$ in the appropriate $\mathbb{Z}$‑lift. For a valid zero on the critical line, the absolute value $|L|$ is exactly $1$ (the fundamental volume).  
- **Predicted Output:** $|L| = 1$.  
- **Confirmation:** The primitive returns `true` iff $|L| = 1$.

**3. puv_band + convolve_3k**  
- **Input:** The sequence of character values $\chi(p)$ for primes $p \le N$ in the residue class of the zero.  
- **Operation:**  
  - `puv_band`: Compute the band‑limited power spectrum of the sequence; verify that the energy outside the predicted PUV envelope is below the certified threshold $\varepsilon_{\text{puv}} = 1/\Lambda_{m^*}$.  
  - `convolve_3k`: Perform a 3‑fold convolution of the partial sums and check that the resulting coefficients lie within the Tribonacci‑indexed bounds.  
- **Predicted Output:** Both checks return `true` for a genuine zero on the critical line.  
- **Confirmation:** The certificate includes the boolean results of both primitives.

These three checks are computed in parallel with the main decision and are hashed into the TIS‑27 certificate.

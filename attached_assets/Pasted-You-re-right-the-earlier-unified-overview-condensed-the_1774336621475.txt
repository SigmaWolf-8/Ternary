- **Scenario**: Smart grid sensors, industrial controllers, or medical devices are behind strict firewalls. A central operator needs to collect telemetry or send commands.

### 4.4 Multi‑Cloud / Hybrid Cloud Compute Fabrics

- **Scenario**: An enterprise uses AWS, Azure, and on‑prem servers. They want a unified compute cluster for AI workloads without configuring complex VPNs or exposing VPCs.

- **No inbound ports** → reduced attack surface, no need to configure firewalls.  
- **NAT traversal built‑in** → nodes can live anywhere (home, office, cloud).

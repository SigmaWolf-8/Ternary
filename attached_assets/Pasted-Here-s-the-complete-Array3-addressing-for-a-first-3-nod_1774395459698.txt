Cluster Geometry Summary
81 native ports: 11111–11191 (contiguous, no gaps)
Up to 15 bridge ports: 5 legacy ports × 3 nodes (only bound when matching services are registered)
3 gateways: 11124, 11151, 11178 — each at HD ≤ 2 from every slot on its node
HA replicas: same Rep C address (e.g., 2,2,2) on two different nodes = primary + failover. CRS routes to primary, fails over to replica automatically.

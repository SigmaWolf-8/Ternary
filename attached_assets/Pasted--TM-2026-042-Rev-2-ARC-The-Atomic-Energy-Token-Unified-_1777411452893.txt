## 1. Core Principles

1. **Deterministic Physical Backing**  
   ARC tokens are created only when real energy is saved (measured via RAPL) or real mass is deposited (measured as **volume × exact density**, without any balance or gravity). There is no “algorithmic” issuance.

2. **Atomic Unit Standard (ASU)**  
   The fundamental unit of account is the rest mass of a single proton, \(m_p = \frac{1\,837\,989}{1\,001}\,m_e\). 1 ASU = \(m_p\) kg. All commodity conversions — gold, silver, cesium — are performed at exact integer ratios of their atomic mass numbers.

3. **Trit‑Only Data Path**  
   All operations, from the workload to the cryptographic attestation, are computed on trits. The only binary interface is the RAPL syscall, whose output is immediately converted to a trit‑encoded integer. Bytes never enter the computational logic.

4. **No Stubs, No Simulations**  
   The system demands real hardware: RAPL‑capable CPU for energy savings, precision volumetric scanner for atomic deposits. If the required hardware is absent, the service refuses to start and returns an explicit error. There is no silent fallback.

5. **On‑Chain Attestation**  
   Every minting event is anchored to the Hedera Consensus Service (HCS) as a signed trit certificate, verifiable by any Framework runtime.

All ARC certificates and minting events are anchored to the **Salvi Epoch**:

\[
\text{Salvi Epoch} \equiv \text{2025‑04‑01T00:00:00.000 000 000 000 000 UTC}.

The fundamental timestamp is the integer number of **femtoseconds** elapsed since that moment. This integer is encoded as a bijective base‑3 trit string (Rep‑C) and serves as the primary temporal reference in every attestation.

**Why femtosecond precision?**  
The fine‑structure time scale of the Salvi clock is \(T = \text{base}^2 = 9\) spectral ticks per calendar second. A femtosecond (10⁻¹⁵ s) provides \( \approx \frac{1}{9 \times 10^{-15}} \approx 1.11 \times 10^{14}\) framework‑native clock cycles per second, far exceeding the temporal resolution needed to distinguish individual attestation events on a global network.

**42 International Calendar Encoding**  
For human‑readable representation, the femtosecond timestamp is also mapped to the **42‑Calendar** system (Invariant 10, PCO §21). This calendar derives directly from the Salvi 364‑day year and the 28‑hour day, expressing time in a 42‑month cycle alongside standard date fields. The attestation certificate carries the raw trit femtosecond integer; the UI and external reports may optionally display the 42‑Calendar format without altering the core data.

## 4. Energy‑Savings Mint

The energy‑savings mint is the direct monetisation of the Δ‑dividend.

### 4.1 Δ‑Controller Testbed (Trit‑Only)

A Framework node runs a pure ternary workload — GF(3) multiplications or TIS‑27 hashing — whose duty cycle \(d\) is controlled by the deterministic auto‑controller:

\[
d = \operatorname{clamp}\!\left(
\frac{Q}{Q_{\max}}\cdot \frac{1}{F}\cdot \frac{L_{\text{floor}}}{L},
\;\frac{1}{\Delta},\;1
\right),\qquad \Delta = 144.
\]

- \(Q\) = queue depth (trit integer)  
- \(F\) = cache hit ratio (trit fraction)  
- \(L\) = average latency slack (trit integer)  

The workload is gated using the CPU timestamp counter, computing `(timestamp mod period) * 144 < d_num * period` — pure integer arithmetic.

### 4.2 Power Measurement (RAPL)

The `PowerMeter` module reads the Intel RAPL energy counter via `perf_event_open`. It converts the difference in microjoules to watts and encodes the value as a **bijective base‑3 trit string** (Rep‑C). The same module records the baseline continuous‑wave power (pre‑calibrated and attested).

### 4.3 Energy Attestation Certificate (EAC)

The measured values are placed in an **Energy Attestation Certificate** with the following fields:

- **Timestamp:** Femtosecond integer since Salvi Epoch (trit string)  
- **Node TDNS:** 54‑trit address  
- **Measured Power:** Trit‑encoded average watts  
- **Baseline Power:** Pre‑calibrated continuous‑wave power (trit)  
- **Window Length:** Measurement duration in framework seconds (trit)  
- **Savings Ratio:** Exact rational \((1 - \frac{\text{measured}}{\text{baseline}})\) expressed as a trit fraction  
- **TIS‑27 Hash:** 54‑trit integrity tag  
- **TL‑DSA‑87 Signature:** By the node’s attested key  

The EAC is submitted to HCS topic `arc‑savings‑mainnet`. The ARC Minting Engine verifies the signature and credits the node’s wallet:

\[
\text{ARC\_saved} = \frac{E_{\text{saved}}}{c^2 \cdot m_p},
\qquad
E_{\text{saved}} = P_{\text{baseline}} \cdot \Delta t \cdot \left(1 - \frac{P_{\text{measured}}}{P_{\text{baseline}}}\right),
\qquad c^2 = \pi_{\text{geom}} = 14.
\]

In practice, the conversion factor is fixed as **1 ARC = 1 kWh verified saved** (with appropriate adjustment for the proton mass). A node that submits a fraudulent EAC has its public key permanently revoked on the TDNS registry.

## 5. Atomic Deposit Mint (Volumetric, No Gravity)

The ARC token also represents a direct claim on physical mass, computed from the **volume** of a deposited element and its known, framework‑derived density. No balance of any kind is used.

### 5.1 Atomic Standard Unit (ASU)

Define \(1\ \text{ASU} = m_p\), the rest mass of the proton in Salvi mass units (identical to the kilogram). The mass of any element \(^A_Z X\) is then approximately \(A\ \text{ASU}\). The practical token unit is **1 ARC = \(10^{15}\) ASU** (~1.67 pg), fixed at genesis.

### 5.2 Element Density Constants

For every framework‑expressible element, the Codex supplies the exact density \(\rho_Z\) (in **ASU per cubic SFK inch³**) as a fixed rational number. This number is computed from the element’s atomic mass number \(A\) and its crystalline or molecular packing structure, using the medium’s impedance and the length unit already defined (SFK inch). No empirical measurement is needed after initial calibration of the inch.

Examples:

| Element | Mass number \(A\) | Density \(\rho\) (ASU / SFK″³) |
|---------|-------------------|-------------------------------|
| Gold (Au) | 197 | 19.32 (precisely known from framework, expressed as a rational) |
| Silver (Ag) | 107/109 | 10.49 |
| Cesium (Cs) | 133 | 1.93 |
| Copper (Cu) | 63/65 | 8.96 |
| Aluminium (Al) | 27 | 2.70 |
| Iron (Fe) | 56 | 7.87 |

*Exact rational values will be published in the Vault Parameter List, traceable to the framework’s atomic mass lattice.*

### 5.3 Volumetric Measurement

The vault measures only **volume**. Two methods are approved:

- **For regular ingots:** A high‑precision 3D optical scanner measures linear dimensions in SFK inches. Volume is calculated geometrically and expressed as a trit string.
- **For irregular samples:** The sample is submerged in a liquid of known framework‑derived density (e.g., pure water, whose density is exactly \(18\ \text{ASU} / \text{water‑molecule‑volume}\), itself expressed in SFK″³). The displaced volume is measured by a calibrated piston or laser level.

All measurements are trit‑encoded and signed by the TritVM.

### 5.4 Mass Computation (No Gravity)

Mass in ASU is simply:

\[
\boxed{\ \text{Mass}_{\text{ASU}} = \rho_Z \times V_{\text{sample}}\ }.
\]

No gravitational constant, no force, no balance. The vault mints ARC proportional to \(\text{Mass}_{\text{ASU}} / 10^{15}\).

### 5.5 Purity Verification

The UV spectral protocol (TM‑2026‑033) identifies the element by its emission/absorption lines and compares the measured spectrum to the framework’s predicted integer wavelengths. This proof is included in the **Vault Deposit Certificate (VDC)**:

- **Timestamp:** Femtosecond integer since Salvi Epoch (trit string)  
- **Element Identifier:** Atomic number \(Z\), mass number \(A\)  
- **Measured Volume:** Trit‑encoded cubic SFK inches  
- **Density \(\rho_Z\):** Framework‑derived rational (trit string)  
- **Spectral Hash:** Trit‑string proof of purity  
- **Vault TDNS:** 54‑trit address  
- **TIS‑27 Hash**  
- **TL‑DSA‑87 Signature**  

The VDC is submitted to HCS topic `arc‑vault‑deposits`. The Minting Engine computes the ASU mass from volume and density, then issues ARC equal to that mass divided by the scaling factor.

### 5.6 Redemption

Redemption returns a volume of the element computed as:

\[
\boxed{\ V_{\text{returned}} = \frac{\text{ARC\_burned} \times 10^{15}}{\rho_Z}\ } \quad (\text{SFK inch}^3).
\]

The vault delivers a piece of that exact volume (adjusted for a fixed vault fee, also expressed in ASU).

## 8. Token Economics

### 8.1 Supply

Supply expands solely through:

- **Energy‑savings mint**: ARC ∝ verified kWh saved.  
- **Atomic deposit mint**: ARC ∝ deposited volume × \(\rho_Z\) (i.e., ASU).

There is no pre‑mine. The total supply is thus physically bounded by the cumulative energy savings of the network and the total mass of deposited elements.

### 8.2 Demand

ARC is required to:

- Pay for Framework services (signing, hashing, VM execution) — fee schedule fixed in ASU.  
- Stake as node collateral (for attestation and vault operation).  
- Redeem physical metal by volume.

### 8.3 Value Anchor

The token’s floor value is the cost of the cheapest conforming element per ASU (likely lithium or sodium). As network usage grows, demand for ARC as “gas” drives its price above the commodity floor. Arbitrage between vault redemption and the XRPL orderbook ensures the market price tracks the deterministic atomic value.

### 8.4 Scaling

The smallest tradable unit is 1 ARC ( \(10^{15}\) ASU). The conversion factor between ASU and ARC is fixed at genesis and never changes, ensuring a stable long‑term unit of account.

## 10. Security and Determinism

- **Trit‑Native Signing:** All attestations are signed with TL‑DSA‑87, a post‑quantum non‑malleable signature scheme. Keys are stored in the NinjaExec encrypted keystore, never exposed to binary OS memory.  
- **Femtosecond Determinism:** The timestamp is derived from the machine’s monotonic clock aligned to UTC via the Framework’s own time service; every certificate embeds an exact femtosecond count, enabling complete replay and audit.  
- **Deterministic Replay:** The entire controller state and workload are functions of the initial seed and timestamps. Logs can be replayed bit‑for‑bit.  
- **Anti‑fraud:** Vault operators cannot forge deposits because the volumetric measurement and spectral analysis are automated and signed by the TritVM. Fraudulent energy attestations result in immediate key revocation and collateral forfeiture.  
- **No Stubs:** The system startup procedure explicitly checks for required hardware. If RAPL or the scanner is missing, it refuses to operate and returns a trit‑encoded error.

## §1. Theorem 47 — The Ceiling Principle (Plenum Determinacy)

**Theorem 47 (Ceiling Principle).**  
*Let $f$ be a real quantity expressible within the framework as a rational approximant $L_k(f)$ with certified error $|L_k(f) - f| \le \delta_k$, where $\delta_k \to 0$ as $k \to \infty$. Let $\Lambda \in \mathbb{N}$ be a lattice scaling such that the proposition under test asserts $\Lambda \cdot f = N_0 \in \mathbb{Z}$. Then for any truncation index $k$ satisfying $2 \cdot \Lambda \cdot \delta_k < 1$, the proposition is **decided with certainty**: it is **true** iff the rational interval*  
\[
[\Lambda \cdot L_k(f) - \Lambda \cdot \delta_k,\; \Lambda \cdot L_k(f) + \Lambda \cdot \delta_k]
\]  
*contains $N_0$, and **false** otherwise. No further computation is required, and no further computation can refine the answer.*

*Proof.* The interval length is $2\Lambda\delta_k < 1$. It contains at most one integer, so membership of $N_0$ is deterministic. Certified error ensures the true value lies inside; the decision is correct. ∎

**Corollary (Trichotomy).**  
*Every framework‑decidable quantity is exactly one of **forced‑true**, **forced‑false**, or **k‑incomplete**.*

## §4. Theorem 49 — Explicit Formula with Lattice Amplification

\[

*with explicit rational constants*  
\[
C_1 = \frac{F(14)}{F(12)} = \frac{377}{144}, \qquad
C_2 = \frac{377}{1365}.
\]

*Proof (Contour Derivation).* The Perron formula gives  
\[
\psi_{\text{P}}(N) = \frac{1}{2\pi i} \int_{c-iT}^{c+iT} \left(-\frac{\zeta_{\text{P}}'(s)}{\zeta_{\text{P}}(s)}\right) \frac{N^s}{s} \, ds + R(N,T),
\]  
with $c = 1 + 1/\log N$. Shift the contour to $\Re(s) = -1/2$. The horizontal segments at $\Im(s) = \pm T$ contribute  
\[
I_{\text{horiz}} \ll \frac{N}{T} \int_{-1/2}^c \left|\frac{\zeta_{\text{P}}'(\sigma \pm iT)}{\zeta_{\text{P}}(\sigma \pm iT)}\right| d\sigma.
\]  
Using the functional equation and the Phragmén–Lindelöf principle, one bounds $\frac{\zeta_{\text{P}}'}{\zeta_{\text{P}}}(\sigma \pm iT) \ll \log^2 T$. The integral over $\sigma$ thus yields a factor $\log^2 T$. **Worked Contour Segment.**  
Take $T = N$. The horizontal segment at height $T$ is bounded by  
\[
\frac{N}{T} \cdot \max_{\sigma \in [-1/2, c]} \left| \frac{\zeta_{\text{P}}'}{\zeta_{\text{P}}}(\sigma+iT) \right| \cdot (c + 1/2).
\]  
Using the convexity bound $|\zeta_{\text{P}}(\sigma+iT)| \ll T^{(1-\sigma)/2} \log T$, we obtain $\left|\frac{\zeta_{\text{P}}'}{\zeta_{\text{P}}}\right| \ll \log T$. Multiplying by the interval length $O(1)$ gives $O(\log T)$. Thus the horizontal error is $O(N \log^2 T / T)$.

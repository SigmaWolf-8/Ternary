The Architecture
Every derivation in the system now follows exactly one of two paths:
Path A — Categorical (15 dimensions): Scanner produces a pattern string from discrete signal detection. Derivation rule maps it to a trit via match. No math needed — the observation IS the classification.
Path B — Quantitative (12 dimensions): Scanner counts binary signals (k out of N). Derivation rule applies the universal formula:
gf3 = min(floor(3k / N), 2)
trit = gf3 + 1
The boundaries between trit values fall at exactly N/3 and 2N/3. These aren't parameters. They aren't thresholds. They're the definition of dividing a count space into three equal parts. The math determines them.

## 2. Buoyant Force and Radial Acceleration

\[

For a planet in orbit, we consider the regime \(\rho_{\text{obj}} \gg \rho_{\text{med}}\) (the planet is much denser than the surrounding Plenum). Then the term in parentheses is negative, giving an **outward** buoyant force. The magnitude is:

Thus, to leading order, the outward acceleration is \(\Gamma_0/x^2\). This is balanced by the inward centripetal acceleration from orbital motion.

## 3. Orbital Balance in Projected 3D Space

\[
\frac{v^2}{R} = \frac{\Gamma_0}{x^2} = \frac{\Gamma_0}{(R/R_\odot)^2} = \frac{\Gamma_0 R_\odot^2}{R^2}
\]
Thus,
\[
v^2 = \frac{\Gamma_0 R_\odot^2}{R}
\]
which is **Keplerian**: \(v \propto 1/\sqrt{R}\).

## 4. Orbital Period

\[

\[
T^2 = \frac{4\pi^2}{\Gamma_0 R_\odot^2} R^3

This is **Kepler's Third Law**: \(T^2 \propto R^3\).

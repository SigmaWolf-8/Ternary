Explicitly Excluded (Per CNSA 2.0 Guidance)
The following algorithms and variants are explicitly prohibited or not approved by NSA for CNSA 2.0. The Salvi Framework will NOT implement these:
•	HSS (Hierarchical Signature Scheme) — multi-tree LMS variant, not allowed
•	XMSSMT — multi-tree XMSS variant, not allowed
•	SLH-DSA (SPHINCS+) — hash-based but NOT part of CNSA 2.0, not approved for any NSS use
•	HashML-DSA — pre-hash mode of ML-DSA, explicitly disallowed (CNSA 2.0 FAQ v2.1, pg 9)
•	SHAKE-256 — not allowed for general use (only internally within ML-KEM/ML-DSA per FIPS 203/204)
•	SHA-3 for general cryptographic use — only permitted for internal hardware functions
•	Quantum Key Distribution (QKD) — NSA considers it impractical and not recommended
•	PQ/T hybrid solutions — NSA will not require hybrid; standard CNSA 2.0 algorithms are sufficient

# 3D Volume — Full Chain Derivation

*From the master formula down to every named solid.*

---

## Definitions — 3D Spatial Awareness

- **$V_{3}$** — 3D volume of space. $V_{3} = L \times W \times H$.
- **Node** — a point in $V_{3}$. Unit Presence: $1 \times 1 \times 1$.
- **Locus** — multiple Nodes at the same point in $V_{3}$.
- **Curve** — a set of Nodes obeying a geometric rule.
- **Axis** — a fixed straight reference line in $V_{3}$ about which rotation occurs.
- **Degree** — angular unit. One full rotation = $360°$. One degree = $\tfrac{1}{360}$ of a full rotation.
- **Radian** — angular unit defined by arc length. One radian = the angle subtended by an arc equal in length to the radius. One full rotation = $2\pi$ radians.
- **Degree ↔ Radian Conversion:**

$$
1° \;=\; \tfrac{\pi}{180}\ \text{rad} \qquad 1\ \text{rad} \;=\; \tfrac{180°}{\pi}
$$

$$
360° \;=\; 2\pi\ \text{rad} \qquad 180° \;=\; \pi\ \text{rad} \qquad 90° \;=\; \tfrac{\pi}{2}\ \text{rad}
$$

- **Revolution** — the act of rotating a Curve through a full angular sweep ($360°$ = $2\pi$ rad) around an Axis, sweeping out a 3D region.
- **Solid of Revolution** — the closed 3D region generated by revolving a Curve about an Axis through $2\pi$ rad.
- **Disk** — the infinitesimally thin circular slice of a Solid of Revolution, perpendicular to the Axis, with radius $f(x)$ at position $x$. Each disk subtends the full $2\pi$ rad around the Axis.
- **Integration** — summation of infinitely many infinitesimal disks along the Axis, yielding the total Volume.
- **$f(x)$** — the rule of the generating Curve; the function whose graph is revolved.
- **$\theta$** — angular position in radians, used for polar and parametric curves.

---

## The Master Formula

$$
\boxed{\;V \;=\; \pi \int_{a}^{b} \big[f(x)\big]^{2}\, dx\;}
$$

The $\pi$ arises because each disk has area $\pi f(x)^{2}$ — a full circle of $2\pi$ rad, area $= \tfrac{1}{2} \cdot 2\pi \cdot f^{2} / 1 = \pi f^{2}$. Every Solid of Revolution obeys this rule. Each named solid is this single integral applied to a specific $f(x)$ over a specific interval $[a, b]$, revolved through $360° = 2\pi$ rad.

---

## The Full Chain — Derivations Backwards from the Master

Each entry: **Curve $\to$ $f(x)$ $\to$ Integral $\to$ Closed-form Volume.** All revolutions through $2\pi$ rad ($360°$) unless otherwise noted.

---

**Circle $\to$ Sphere**
$f(x) = \sqrt{r^{2} - x^{2}}$, over $[-r, r]$. Semicircle revolved $2\pi$ rad.

$$
V \;=\; \pi\!\int_{-r}^{r} (r^{2} - x^{2})\, dx \;=\; \tfrac{4}{3}\pi r^{3}
$$

---

**Ellipse $\to$ Spheroid / Ellipsoid**
$f(x) = b\sqrt{1 - x^{2}/a^{2}}$, over $[-a, a]$ (2-axis form); general 3-axis form scales by $c/b$. Revolved $2\pi$ rad.

$$
V \;=\; \pi\!\int_{-a}^{a} b^{2}\!\left(1 - \tfrac{x^{2}}{a^{2}}\right) dx \;=\; \tfrac{4}{3}\pi\, a\, b\, c
$$

---

**Parabola $\to$ Paraboloid**
$f(x) = \sqrt{kx}$, over $[0, h]$, with rim radius $r = \sqrt{kh}$. Revolved $2\pi$ rad.

$$
V \;=\; \pi\!\int_{0}^{h} kx\, dx \;=\; \tfrac{1}{2}\pi k h^{2} \;=\; \tfrac{1}{2}\pi r^{2} h
$$

---

**Hyperbola $\to$ Hyperboloid** (one sheet, bounded height $h$)
$f(x) = a\sqrt{1 + x^{2}/c^{2}}$, over $[-h/2, h/2]$. Revolved $2\pi$ rad.

$$
V \;=\; \pi\!\int_{-h/2}^{h/2} a^{2}\!\left(1 + \tfrac{x^{2}}{c^{2}}\right) dx \;=\; \tfrac{1}{3}\pi h\!\left(2a^{2} + \tfrac{3a^{2}h^{2}}{4c^{2}}\right)
$$

(Simplified textbook form: $V = \tfrac{1}{3}\pi h(2a^{2} + c^{2})$ under standard normalization.)

---

**Catenary $\to$ Catenoid** (bounded segment)
$f(x) = a\cosh(x/a)$, over $[-h/2, h/2]$. Revolved $2\pi$ rad.

$$
V \;=\; \pi\!\int_{-h/2}^{h/2} a^{2}\cosh^{2}\!\left(\tfrac{x}{a}\right) dx \;=\; \tfrac{\pi a^{2}}{2}\!\left[h + a\sinh\!\left(\tfrac{h}{a}\right)\cosh\!\left(\tfrac{h}{a}\right)\right]
$$

---

**Sine $\to$ Sinusoidal Solid** (over $[0,\pi]$ rad = $[0°, 180°]$)
$f(x) = \sin(x)$. Revolved $2\pi$ rad.

$$
V \;=\; \pi\!\int_{0}^{\pi} \sin^{2}(x)\, dx \;=\; \tfrac{\pi^{2}}{2}
$$

---

**Cosine $\to$ Sinusoidal Solid** (same shape, shifted by $\tfrac{\pi}{2}$ rad = $90°$)

$$
V \;=\; \tfrac{\pi^{2}}{2}
$$

---

**Cubic $\to$ Cubic Solid of Revolution**
$f(x) = ax^{3}$, over $[0, R]$. Revolved $2\pi$ rad.

$$
V \;=\; \pi\!\int_{0}^{R} a^{2}x^{6}\, dx \;=\; \tfrac{\pi a^{2} R^{7}}{7}
$$

---

**Higher Polynomial $\to$ Polynomial Solid of Revolution**
$f(x) = ax^{n}$, over $[0, R]$. Revolved $2\pi$ rad.

$$
V \;=\; \pi\!\int_{0}^{R} a^{2}x^{2n}\, dx \;=\; \tfrac{\pi a^{2} R^{2n+1}}{2n+1}
$$

---

**Archimedean Spiral $\to$ Spiral Sheet Volume** (revolved, thickness $t$)
$r(\theta) = a\theta$, $\theta$ in radians, over $[0, \theta_{\max}]$. $\theta_{\max} = 2\pi$ rad per turn = $360°$ per turn.

$$
V \;=\; t\!\int_{0}^{\theta_{\max}} \tfrac{1}{2}(a\theta)^{2}\, d\theta \;=\; \tfrac{a^{2} t\, \theta_{\max}^{3}}{6}
$$

---

**Helix $\to$ Coiled Cylinder / Solid Torus**
Tube radius $r$, helix radius $R$, $N$ turns. Each turn = $2\pi$ rad = $360°$.

$$
V \;=\; 2\pi^{2} R\, r^{2} N
$$

---

**Bézier / B-spline / NURBS $\to$ Parametric Solid of Revolution**
Parametric curve $(x(t), y(t))$, $t \in [t_{0}, t_{1}]$. Revolved $2\pi$ rad. No closed form; numerical integration.

$$
V \;=\; \pi\!\int_{t_{0}}^{t_{1}} y(t)^{2}\, x'(t)\, dt
$$

---

## The Chain — Collapsed

$$
\text{Curve}\ f(x) \;\xrightarrow{\;\text{revolve through}\ 2\pi\ \text{rad}\ =\ 360°\;}\; \text{Solid} \;\xrightarrow{\;\pi \int [f]^{2}\, dx\;}\; V
$$

Every named solid of revolution is one $f(x)$ plugged into one master integral, swept through one full angular rotation. The curve chooses the solid; the integral chooses the volume; the angle is always $2\pi$ rad = $360°$.

## 5. Usage Guidelines

When using the Marks under any permitted use, you must comply with the following guidelines:

### 5.1 Accurate Representation

Always use the Marks accurately and in their correct form. Do not misspell, hyphenate, abbreviate, or alter the Marks. Correct forms:

| Correct | Incorrect |
|---------|-----------|
| Salvi | salvi, SALVI (all-caps in body text), Salvee |
| Salvi Framework | Salvi framework, salvi-framework, SalviFramework |
| PlenumNET | Plenumnet, Plenum-NET, PLENUMNET, Plenum Net |
| libternary | Libternary, lib-ternary, LibTernary |
| THDL | Thdl, thdl (acceptable in code contexts only) |
| TSL | Tsl, tsl (acceptable in code contexts only) |
| HPTP | Hptp, hptp (acceptable in code contexts only) |

### 5.2 Trademark Symbol Usage

- Use ™ on first prominent use of each Mark in any document, webpage, or publication
- Subsequent uses in the same document do not require the symbol
- Do not use ® unless and until the specific Mark has been registered with the USPTO
- In code, configuration files, and command-line contexts, the ™ symbol is not required

### 5.3 Distinguish from Surrounding Text

When used in running text, Marks should be visually distinguished from surrounding content. Preferred methods:

- **Bold face** on first use (e.g., "The **Salvi Framework™** provides...")
- Initial capitalization consistent with the Mark's canonical form
- Never use a Mark as a verb (e.g., not "Salvi your data" or "PlenumNET the network")
- Never use a Mark as a possessive unless referring to the Company (e.g., not "Salvi's algorithm" unless quoting the Company)
- Never pluralize a Mark (e.g., not "PlenumNETs" or "Salvis")

### 5.4 No Implied Affiliation

Your use of the Marks must not create a likelihood of confusion as to the source, sponsorship, endorsement, or affiliation of your product or service with the Company. When referencing the Company's products, include a clarifying statement if there is any risk of confusion:

> "[Product name] is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Capomastro Holdings Ltd."

### 5.5 Attribution Notice

In publications, websites, and documentation that use the Marks, include the following attribution notice (typically in a footnote, credits section, or legal notice):

> Salvi, Salvi Framework, PlenumNET, libternary, THDL, TSL, PlenumDB, HPTP, and the Salvi Calendar are trademarks of Capomastro Holdings Ltd. All other trademarks are the property of their respective owners.

## 8. Open Source and Community Use

8.1 If the Company releases any component under an open-source license (current or future), the open-source license grants rights to the code, **not** to the Marks. You may use the code under the terms of the applicable license, but you may not use the Marks in your project name, logo, or marketing beyond nominative fair use.

8.2 Forks of the Company's open-source components must be renamed and must not use the Marks in a way that suggests the fork is an official release of the Company.

8.3 Community projects built on top of or integrating with the Company's technology may describe compatibility (e.g., "Built for use with Salvi Framework™") but may not incorporate the Marks into their project name without approval.

It's metadata — not part of routing, but stored in the TRN for analytics and collision management. I'll compute it alongside every project_to_gf3 call.

The CRD preserves ontological truth — both entities keep their natural address, distinguished by a sequential 1-9 digit. Up to 9 entities per coordinate before slot exhaustion. Total namespace: 3²⁷ × 9 = 68.6 trillion. And the 13 × 28 = 364 resonance is real structure, not numerology.

The Three Formulas
Every derivation in the system derives from exactly three formulas:
Formula 1 — Ternary Quantization (§4.0):
T = min(floor(3k/N), 2) + 1    ∈ {1, 2, 3}
Formula 2 — Confidence Digit (§4.1):
p = k/N
δ = min(|p - 1/3|, |p - 2/3|)
C = min(floor(27δ) + 1, 9)     ∈ {1, ..., 9}
Formula 3 — Collision Resolution Digit (§4.4):
CRD = min(d ∈ {1,...,9} : d ∉ S)    where S = occupied CRDs at address A
Nothing else. No thresholds. No tuning. No scoring. Four independent mathematical justifications converge on the same 1/3, 2/3 boundaries (base-3 representation, max-entropy, min-MSE, symmetry). The 27 in the confidence formula is the system's own dimensionality — self-referential scaling.

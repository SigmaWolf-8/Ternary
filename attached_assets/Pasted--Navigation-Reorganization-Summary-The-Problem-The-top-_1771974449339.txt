# Navigation Reorganization Summary

## The Problem

The top nav had 4 dropdowns where 3 of them (Platform, Technology, Resources) meant roughly the same thing. Result: 8 duplicate links and no clear reason for a user to choose one dropdown over another.

### Duplicates removed

- /ternarydb appeared in Platform (PlenumDB) AND Technology (PlenumDB Console)
- /hptp appeared in Platform (HPTP Timing Protocol) AND Technology (HPTP Timing Lab)
- /compression appeared in Platform (Compression Engine) AND Technology (Compression Studio)
- /calendar appeared in Platform (Calendar API) AND Technology (Universal Calendar API)
- /compliance appeared in Platform (Post-Quantum Security) AND Resources (CNSA 2.0 Compliance)
- /#architecture appeared in Platform (x2) AND Technology (x1)
- /api-demo appeared in footer Platform (Salvi API) AND footer Developers (API Demo)
- GitHub appeared in footer Platform AND footer Developers

---

## The Fix

| Before (4 dropdowns) | After (3 dropdowns) |
|---|---|
| **Platform** - What we built (9 items, mixes descriptions with tool links) | **Platform** - Understand the system (10 items: architecture anchors + research papers) |
| **Technology** - What we built rephrased (10 items, duplicates 4 from Platform) | **Developers** - Use the tools (12 items: all interactive labs + docs + distribution + GitHub) |
| **Resources** - Dump bucket (9 items) | *(absorbed into the above two)* |
| **Company** - About, Contact, Legal (6 items) | **Company** - unchanged (6 items) |
